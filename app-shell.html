Built for people who actually work

Deskloop is a free suite of browser-based productivity tools designed for focused, intentional work. Every tool loads instantly, requires no account, and solves one problem clearly — then gets out of your way.

The philosophy is simple: the best productivity tool disappears after you use it. No dashboards to manage, no onboarding flows, no upsells. Just the tool, doing exactly what it says it does.

New tools ship regularly. Everything on Deskloop is and will always remain completely free to use. Built by a solo maker who got tired of bloated productivity apps.
