Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 May 2026  ·  Applies to deskloop.work and all its tools

The short version: Deskloop collects no personal data. We have no accounts, no tracking, no cookies of our own, and no backend. Everything you do in any Deskloop tool stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Deskloop (deskloop.work) is a free suite of browser-based productivity tools. You can contact us at hello@deskloop.work.

2. What data we collect

Personal data: none. Deskloop does not collect, store, or process any personal information. We have no user accounts, no registration forms, and no login system.

Tool data: stays on your device. All data generated by Deskloop tools — focus sessions, habits, work hours, streaks, notes, settings — is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

3. Cookies and Consent

Deskloop itself sets no first-party cookies. We do not use analytics cookies, tracking cookies, or session cookies of our own.

3a. Legal basis for processing (GDPR Article 6)

We process personal data in connection with advertising cookies on the basis of consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR). You provide consent by clicking Accept on the cookie banner. You may withdraw consent at any time as described in section 3b. Where you decline, no advertising cookies are set and no personal data is processed through our site for advertising purposes.

3b. Google AdSense advertising cookies

Google AdSense serves advertisements on Deskloop. Before AdSense loads, we ask for your consent through the banner shown at the bottom of the page. AdSense scripts are only loaded after you click Accept. If you click Decline, no AdSense scripts are loaded and no advertising cookies are set during that session.

When you accept, Google may set cookies on your device to serve relevant advertising based on your browsing activity, including across other websites. These are third-party cookies controlled entirely by Google, not by Deskloop.

3c. Managing and withdrawing consent

You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage for deskloop.work. The GDPR banner will reappear on your next visit, allowing you to choose again.

To opt out of Google's personalised advertising regardless of your choice here, visit adssettings.google.com. To understand how Google uses data when you use our site, see How Google uses information from sites that use our services.

You can also install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on and use browser settings to block or delete cookies at any time.

4. Third-party services

Deskloop loads resources from the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — to load the Inter and JetBrains Mono typefaces. Google may log the request. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • jsDelivr / cdnjs — used in the Time Zone Overlap tool to load world map data and charting libraries. These are static file CDNs and do not track users.
  • Google AdSense — for displaying advertisements. See section 3 above.

No other third-party services, analytics platforms, or tracking scripts are loaded on Deskloop.

5. Analytics

Deskloop does not use Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Hotjar, or any other analytics service. We do not track page views, sessions, or user behavior.

Cloudflare, our DNS and CDN provider, may collect aggregate, anonymized traffic data (such as total request counts and geographic region) as part of its standard infrastructure service. This data is not personally identifiable and is not shared with us in identifiable form.

6. Your data and your rights

Because Deskloop collects no personal data, most data protection rights (access, erasure, portability) apply directly to your own browser storage — all your tool data is already in your hands and can be deleted by clearing your browser's localStorage.

Regarding Google AdSense:

  • Right to withdraw consent — clear localStorage for deskloop.work to reset your AdSense consent choice.
  • Right to opt out of personalised ads — visit adssettings.google.com.
  • Right to access Google's data about you — visit myaccount.google.com.

If you are in the European Union, UK, or California and have questions about your privacy rights, contact us at hello@deskloop.work. We respond within 30 days.

7. California residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the CCPA gives you specific rights regarding your personal information.

Google AdSense may share data with advertising partners in ways that qualify as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information under CCPA. You have the right to opt out.

  • Click Decline on the cookie banner when you visit Deskloop.
  • Visit Google Ad Settings to manage personalised advertising across all Google services.
  • Use the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out tool for broader opt-out across participating ad networks.

For other CCPA rights (right to know, right to delete), contact hello@deskloop.work. Deskloop stores no personal data on our servers. Google may hold data collected through AdSense — manage it at myaccount.google.com.
