Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Applies to deskloop.work and all its tools

The short version: Deskloop collects no personal data. We have no accounts, no tracking, no cookies of our own, and no backend. Everything you do in any Deskloop tool stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Deskloop (deskloop.work) is a free suite of browser-based productivity tools. You can contact us at hello@deskloop.work.

2. What data we collect

Personal data: none. Deskloop does not collect, store, or process any personal information. We have no user accounts, no registration forms, and no login system.

Tool data: stays on your device. All data generated by Deskloop tools — focus sessions, habits, work hours, streaks, notes, settings — is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

3. Cookies

Deskloop itself sets no cookies. We do not use analytics cookies, tracking cookies, or preference cookies of any kind.

Google AdSense: Deskloop displays ads served by Google AdSense. Google may set cookies on your device to serve relevant advertising. These are third-party cookies controlled entirely by Google. You can opt out of personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Third-party services

Deskloop loads resources from the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — to load the Inter and JetBrains Mono typefaces. Google may log the request. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • jsDelivr / cdnjs — used in the Time Zone Overlap tool to load world map data and charting libraries. These are static file CDNs and do not track users.
  • Google AdSense — for displaying advertisements. See section 3 above.

No other third-party services, analytics platforms, or tracking scripts are loaded on Deskloop.

5. Analytics

Deskloop does not use Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Hotjar, or any other analytics service. We do not track page views, sessions, or user behavior.

Cloudflare, our DNS and CDN provider, may collect aggregate, anonymized traffic data (such as total request counts and geographic region) as part of its standard infrastructure service. This data is not personally identifiable and is not shared with us in identifiable form.

6. Your data and your rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete, export, or correct on your behalf. Your tool data is stored in your own browser. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for deskloop.work.

If you are in the European Union, UK, or California and have questions about your privacy rights, please contact us at hello@deskloop.work and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's privacy

Deskloop is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Deskloop after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or concerns, contact us at hello@deskloop.work.
