Meeting Cost Calculator Free Online — How Much Do Your Meetings Cost?

The Deskloop Meeting Cost Calculator shows you the real-time dollar cost of your meeting as it runs. Enter your team size and average salary, hit Start, and watch the counter tick up every second. It's a simple, visceral way to understand whether your meetings are worth the time they consume.

How is meeting cost calculated?

Meeting cost is calculated as: number of attendees × average hourly rate × duration in hours. The hourly rate is derived from the annual salary divided by working hours per year (default 2,080 hours, which is 52 weeks × 40 hours). For a 6-person team with an $80,000 average salary, a 60-minute meeting costs approximately $231.

Why does meeting cost matter?

Research by Harvard Business School and MIT consistently shows that meeting overload is one of the largest hidden costs in modern organizations. The average knowledge worker spends over 30% of their working hours in meetings. Making that cost visible — as a live, ticking dollar figure — changes how teams approach scheduling, agenda-setting, and participation.

When to use this calculator

Use it before scheduling a recurring meeting to sanity-check its annual cost. Use it live during a meeting that's running long. Use it to make the case for async communication over synchronous meetings. Share the result card with your team to start a conversation about meeting culture.
