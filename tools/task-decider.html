Random Task Decider Wheel — Beat Decision Paralysis for Free

The Deskloop Task Decider is a free spinning wheel that randomly picks your next task. Add up to 12 tasks to the wheel, hit Spin, and commit to whatever it lands on. No signup, no account, no install — works entirely in your browser and persists your task list across sessions.

Why decision paralysis kills productivity

When every task on your list feels equally important, the brain defaults to inaction or low-value busywork. The mental energy spent deciding what to work on is wasted energy that could go into the work itself. Removing the decision entirely — by delegating it to a spin of the wheel — bypasses overthinking and triggers immediate action.

How to use the Task Decider effectively

Add only tasks that genuinely need doing today. Keep names short and actionable. When the wheel lands on a result, commit to it for at least 25 minutes before questioning it — pair it with the Focus Timer for a complete workflow. Use "Done — remove" to shrink the wheel as you complete tasks, turning it into a satisfying progress tracker.

The psychology behind spinning

Randomness removes ego from the decision. When a wheel picks your next task, there's no internal debate about whether you're choosing the "right" thing — the choice is made. This is similar to the Pomodoro Technique's external time constraint: offloading a cognitive decision to a system reduces friction and gets you started faster.
