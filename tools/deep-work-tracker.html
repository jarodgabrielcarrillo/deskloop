Deep Work Tracker Free — Flow State Sessions with Energy & Focus Ratings

The Deskloop Deep Work Tracker is a free, browser-based tool for logging focused work sessions with genuine self-reflection built in. Clock into a session, choose your category, and when you end it, rate your energy level and focus quality on simple 1–5 emoji scales. Over time this builds a personal dataset of when and how you do your best thinking.

Energy and focus ratings

Two lightweight ratings after every session — Energy Level (💤 Exhausted → ⚡ High Energy) and Focus Quality (😕 Scattered → ✨ Deep Flow) — take under 10 seconds and reveal patterns invisible to raw time tracking. Maybe you focus best at low energy. Maybe high-energy mornings scatter you. The data shows what the clock can't.

Day streak counter

The streak counter tracks consecutive days you complete at least one deep work session. Missing a day resets to zero. Your best-ever streak is preserved permanently. The streak is visible on the hero timer so it's part of the daily ritual — not buried in a dashboard.

Weekly bar chart

The weekly chart colors each day's bar by average focus quality — emerald for deep flow sessions, blue for good work, amber for scattered days. Navigate back through previous weeks to see your consistency over time.
