Work Hours Logger Free — Deep Work Tracker with Streak Counter

The Deskloop Work Hours Logger is a free, browser-based tool for tracking your work sessions with a prominent live timer, daily streak counter, and weekly view. Clock in when you start, add a note and tag, then clock out. Every session is stored locally — no account required, works offline, data never leaves your browser.

Daily streak counter

The streak counter tracks consecutive days you clock in. Missing a day resets the streak to zero. Your best-ever streak is preserved separately, so you can always see your personal record. The streak logic uses a simple check: if you clocked in yesterday and clock in again today, your streak increments by one.

Deep work tracking with tags

Tag every session as Deep Work, Meeting, Admin, Learning, or Break. Each tag has a distinct color accent on the session card. Over time, the weekly view reveals exactly how your hours are actually distributed — how much is focused work versus meetings versus administrative overhead.

Weekly bar chart and CSV export

The weekly view shows a bar chart of hours per day, with today's bar highlighted in emerald. Navigate back through previous weeks. Export any week to CSV for payroll, invoicing, or spreadsheet analysis.

Work Hours Logger — Track Billable Hours by Client and Project

How to log your hours

Click Clock In when you start work on a client or project. Add a label for what you are working on. Click Clock Out when you finish. The session is saved with its duration, date, and label. Review your daily and weekly totals in the charts below.

Billable hours for freelancers

Freelancers billing by the hour need an accurate record of time spent per client. The logger tracks each session separately so you can see exactly how many hours you spent on a project before sending an invoice. Export to CSV to share with a client or import into your invoicing workflow.

Streak counter and weekly charts

The daily streak counter tracks how many consecutive days you have logged work. The weekly bar chart shows your hours per day so you can spot your most and least productive days. Pair the logger with the Work Hours target to see whether you are hitting your billing goals.

Works offline, no account needed

All data saves to your browser's localStorage. The logger works fully offline once the page has loaded. Nothing leaves your device. Export to CSV at any time to keep a permanent record outside the browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the work hours logger record my time?

Hit Start when you begin a task and Stop when you finish. The logger records the exact duration and attaches it to the project or client you selected. You can also add entries manually if you forgot to run the timer — enter the start time, end time, and the logger calculates the duration.

Can I track hours for multiple clients at once?

Yes. Create a separate project or client entry for each account you work on. Switch between them freely throughout the day. The logger keeps each client's hours separate and totals them independently so you can bill accurately without mixing up time across accounts.

Can I add notes to each time entry?

Yes. Each log entry has an optional notes field for task descriptions, deliverable names, or billing references. Notes appear on CSV exports and in the summary view, making it straightforward to turn your log into a detailed timesheet for client invoices.

How do I export my logged hours?

Click Export to download a CSV file containing all your time entries — date, project, duration, hourly rate, total value, and notes. Filter by date range or client before exporting to get exactly the records you need for a specific invoice period.

Can I set an hourly rate per project?

Yes. Assign a different hourly rate to each project. The logger multiplies your logged hours by the rate automatically and shows the earnings total for each client alongside the time totals. Useful for freelancers who charge different rates across different types of work.

Does my log save automatically?

Yes. Every entry saves to localStorage the moment you stop the timer or submit a manual entry. Your full log history persists across browser sessions indefinitely. Nothing sends to any server and no account is required.

How does this pair with the invoice generator?

Export your logged hours as a CSV, then use the values to populate the Invoice Generator on Deskloop. The combination gives you a complete freelance billing workflow — log time, review totals, generate a professional PDF invoice — without any paid software.
