Bill Splitter Free — Split the Bill Calculator

Four ways to split

Equal splits the total evenly. Custom lets each person enter their exact share. Percent assigns a portion of the total by percentage, with a warning if amounts do not add up to 100%. By item lets you add individual dishes or expenses and assign them to specific people — useful when everyone ordered different things at different prices.

Tip, tax, and rounding

Choose a tip percentage from the preset buttons or enter a custom amount. Add tax if your bill does not already include it. Rounding adjusts each person's share up or down to the nearest whole number — useful for cash payments.

Who owes who

Select who paid and the results panel shows each person's debt. Share the breakdown with one tap — the share link encodes the full bill in the URL so anyone with the link sees the same numbers.

Save splits as templates

Save recurring splits — a regular group dinner, a shared subscription, a recurring expense — and reload them instantly next time. Up to 6 templates stored locally in your browser.
