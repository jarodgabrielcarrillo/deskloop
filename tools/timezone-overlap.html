Interactive Timezone Boundary Map — Find Your Overlap

The Deskloop Time Zone Overlap Finder uses actual IANA timezone boundary data from Natural Earth — the same dataset used in professional cartography. Each colored region is a distinct timezone that follows real political and geographic boundaries, not just country borders. Click any region to add it, then instantly see which hours overlap for your entire team.

Built for remote teams scheduling across multiple continents. Free, no signup, no data stored. Works entirely in your browser.
