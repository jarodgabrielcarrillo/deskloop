Interactive Timezone Map — Find Your Overlap

The Deskloop Time Zone Overlap Finder renders a world map divided by actual timezone boundaries. Each colored region represents a UTC offset zone — click any region to add it, then instantly see which hours overlap for your entire team in the 24-hour grid below.

Built for remote teams scheduling across multiple continents. Timezone boundaries are drawn only between zones of different offsets — countries in the same timezone merge visually into one region, just like standard timezone reference maps. Free, no signup, no data stored.
