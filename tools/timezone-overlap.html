Time Zone Overlap Tool — Find Shared Working Hours Instantly

How to find shared hours

Add your time zone and your team members' or clients' time zones. The tool shows the hours where your working days overlap. Green hours are shared business hours for both locations. The visual grid makes it easy to see at a glance when everyone is available.

Daylight saving time handled automatically

DST switching dates differ by country and change the offset between locations by one hour for parts of the year. The calculator uses current, DST-adjusted offsets so your overlap window is always accurate for today's date.

Built for remote teams and freelancers

Remote teams need to find standup times that work across locations. Freelancers need to schedule client calls without asking someone to check their local time. This tool gives you the answer in seconds without manual conversion.

Common combinations explained

New York and London share 9am to 12pm ET during most of the year — a comfortable morning standup. Add Singapore and the window shrinks to early morning US time. Fully global teams spanning 12+ hours have no overlapping business hours and need async-first workflows.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the timezone overlap tool find meeting times?

Add the locations or timezones of each team member and set their working hours. The tool calculates the overlap — the window where everyone's working hours coincide — and highlights those times in green. If no overlap exists, it shows the closest windows and the hours difference between each pair of locations.

Can I compare more than two timezones at once?

Yes. Add as many locations as your team requires. The tool displays all timezones in a side-by-side grid and highlights the shared working window across all of them simultaneously. Useful for distributed teams spanning three or more continents.

Does it adjust for daylight saving time automatically?

Yes. The tool uses your browser's built-in timezone data, which includes current daylight saving time offsets for all supported regions. Times update automatically when DST changes occur so you don't need to manually adjust for seasonal clock shifts.

Can I save my team's timezone configuration?

Yes. Save your team's locations and working hours as a named configuration and reload it any time. Useful for teams with a fixed set of locations that you check regularly. Configurations save to localStorage and load instantly.

What counts as working hours?

You define working hours for each team member. The default is 9 AM to 6 PM local time, but you can adjust it for each location independently. Night-shift workers, part-time collaborators, or people with non-standard hours can each have their own window set.

Can I share my timezone overlap with the team?

Yes. Generate a shareable link with your team's configuration encoded in the URL. Anyone with the link sees the same timezone grid and overlap window without needing to set it up themselves.

Is this free with no account required?

Completely free. No sign-up, no email, no subscription. Open the page, add your team's locations, and find your overlap window immediately. Saved configurations store in your browser and nothing leaves your device.
