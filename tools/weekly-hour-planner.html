Weekly Hour Planner Free — Track Time Against Your Goals

How it works

Add categories for anything you want to track — study subjects, client projects, exercise, reading, creative work. Set a weekly hour target for each. Enter how many hours you spent each day. The progress ring and bars update instantly, and the insights panel tells you where you need to focus.

Heatmap coloring

Each logged cell changes color based on how many hours it contains. Light tint for a short session, deeper green for a heavy day. At a glance you can see which days were productive for each category and which were skipped.

Copy from last week

If your schedule repeats — regular study hours, recurring client work, daily exercise — copy last week's logged hours into the current week with one click. Edit whatever changed.

Smart insights

The insights panel calculates where you stand against each target and suggests how many hours per remaining day you need to hit your goal. It tells you which categories are on track, which are behind, and which are already complete.

Export and history

Export a CSV with all logged hours and targets. Copy a plain-text summary for notes or messages. Every week saves automatically in your browser's localStorage. Your history builds over time and nothing ever leaves your device.
