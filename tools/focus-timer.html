Free Pomodoro Timer Online — No Signup, No Install

The Deskloop Focus Timer is a free Pomodoro timer you can use directly in your browser with no signup and no account required. Start a focus session in under three seconds — just open the page and hit play. All session data is stored locally in your browser and never sent anywhere.

What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method developed by Francesco Cirillo. You work in focused intervals (traditionally 25 minutes), separated by short breaks (5 minutes). After completing four sessions, you take a longer break (15–30 minutes). This rhythm improves sustained concentration and prevents mental fatigue.

Custom intervals for any workflow

The classic 25/5 rhythm works well for most tasks. For deep, uninterrupted work on complex problems, the 50/10 or 90/15 presets extend your focus window. For quick sprints through simple tasks, the 15/3 preset keeps momentum high. You can also set any custom interval manually.

Ambient sounds to block distractions

Research consistently shows that steady background noise — white noise, rain, or café ambient sound — helps mask unpredictable distractions and improves focus for many people. All sounds in this timer are generated directly by your browser using the Web Audio API. No audio files are downloaded.

Works offline, saves locally

Once this page has loaded, the Focus Timer works completely offline. Your session history, streak data, and settings persist across browser sessions via localStorage. Your data is yours — it never leaves your device.
