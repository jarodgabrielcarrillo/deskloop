Final Grade Calculator Free — What Do You Need on Your Final?

How the calculation works

Your overall grade is a weighted average. If the final counts for 30%, your pre-final work counts for 70%. The calculator solves for the exact score you need on the final: (target − current × pre-final weight) ÷ final weight. Enter your numbers and get the answer instantly.

What if the result is over 100%?

A result over 100% means your target grade is not mathematically reachable. Your current standing is too low for the final to make up the difference. Consider adjusting your target grade down or speaking to your professor about extra credit options before the semester ends.

Multiple finals planning

The Multiple Finals tab shows every course side by side. Enter each course name, current grade, and final weight, set one target, and see exactly what you need in each exam. The semester overview shows your overall required average and how many courses are already secured.

Free, no signup, works offline

This calculator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server. Once loaded it works offline, and your data never leaves your device. Saved scenarios persist across browser sessions via localStorage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the final grade calculator work?

Enter each graded component in your course — assignments, midterms, quizzes, projects — along with its weight and your current score. The calculator computes your weighted average and shows what score you need on the final exam to reach your target grade.

What if my components don't add up to 100%?

The calculator flags any weight discrepancy so you can correct it before running the calculation. All component weights must total 100% to produce an accurate result. If your syllabus uses a different scale, adjust the weights proportionally until they sum correctly.

Can I calculate what I need on my final exam to pass?

Yes. Set your target grade — whether that's passing, a B, or a specific GPA threshold — and the calculator shows the exact score you need on the final. If the required score exceeds 100%, you know immediately that the target is mathematically out of reach and can adjust your goal accordingly.

Does it support different grading scales?

Yes. Switch between percentage-based grading and letter grade systems. The calculator converts between scales automatically. You can also set a custom passing threshold if your institution uses a non-standard grading system.

Can I save my courses for the whole semester?

Yes. Save each course as a separate entry with its own component weights and scores. Switch between courses without losing data. Everything saves to localStorage so your records persist across sessions without any account.

How is a weighted average calculated?

Multiply each component score by its weight, then sum all the results. A midterm worth 30% with a score of 80 contributes 24 points to your weighted average. Add all the weighted contributions together and you get your current grade before the final exam.

Is this free with no sign-up?

Completely free. No account, no email, no subscription. Open the page, enter your grades, and get your result instantly. All saved courses stay in your browser's localStorage.
