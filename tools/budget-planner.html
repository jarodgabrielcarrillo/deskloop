Budget Planner Free — Personal Budget Calculator

How to use the budget planner

Add your income sources — salary, freelance, side income — and your expenses by category. The surplus or deficit updates instantly. The donut chart shows where your money goes. Switch between weekly, monthly, and yearly views at any time.

The 50/30/20 rule

The 50/30/20 rule divides income into three buckets: 50% for needs (housing, food, transport, health), 30% for wants (entertainment, subscriptions, dining out), and 20% for savings and investments. The calculator shows your target for each bucket and how far off you are.

Savings goals

Add savings goals with a name, target amount, and how much you currently have saved. The calculator shows your monthly contribution needed to reach the goal at your current surplus rate, and how many months it will take.

Export and share

Copy a plain-text summary to clipboard, export a CSV file for spreadsheets, or print a clean summary. All data stays in your browser's localStorage — nothing leaves your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the budget planner calculate my remaining balance?

Enter your monthly income at the top, then add your expenses by category. The planner subtracts your total expenses from your income in real time and shows your remaining balance, savings rate, and how long it will take to reach your savings goal at the current surplus rate.

Can I track multiple months?

Yes. Each month stores separately in localStorage. Switch between months using the navigation arrows. Your expense categories and income carry forward as defaults so you only need to update the numbers that changed.

Can I set a savings goal?

Yes. Enter a savings target and the planner calculates your progress, the amount remaining, and the estimated months to reach your goal based on your current monthly surplus. Update your income or expenses and the projection adjusts instantly.

Does my budget data stay private?

Completely private. All data saves to your browser's localStorage and never leaves your device. No server receives your income or expense figures. Clear your browser data at any time to erase everything permanently.

Can I export my budget?

Yes. Export a CSV of your income and expenses for any month, or copy a plain-text summary to paste into a message or document. You can also print a clean one-page summary directly from the tool.

Is this suitable for students managing a tight budget?

Yes. The budget planner works for any income level and any currency. Students commonly use it to track allowance, part-time income, tuition payments, and living expenses across the semester. There is no account required and no data leaves the device.

What currencies does it support?

The planner is currency-agnostic. Type your amounts in any currency and the calculations work the same way. The currency symbol field lets you set your preferred symbol — peso, dollar, euro, pound, or anything else — and it appears throughout the interface.
