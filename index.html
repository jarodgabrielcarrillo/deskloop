// Common questions

Everything you might want to know.

Quick answers. No fluff.

Are all Deskloop tools really free? +
Yes — completely free, with no strings attached. Every tool is and will always remain free to use. Deskloop is sustained by optional, non-intrusive ad revenue. There is no premium tier, no upsell, and no freemium limitation.
Do I need to create an account or sign up? +
No account needed. Open any tool and use it immediately. Your habit streaks, work session logs, and settings are saved directly in your browser's localStorage — no server, no account, no email required.
Does Deskloop work offline? +
Yes. Once a page has loaded in your browser, all Deskloop tools work completely offline. Your focus timer keeps running, your habit tracker saves, and your work hours are logged — internet or not.
What happens to my data? +
Absolutely nothing leaves your device. There is no analytics, no tracking, no third-party scripts, and no cookies beyond what your browser itself sets. All data is stored in localStorage on your own machine and is yours entirely.
Can I use Deskloop on mobile? +
Yes. Every tool is designed mobile-first and tested on both iOS and Android. No app download required — just open deskloop.work in any mobile browser and use it immediately.
Will Deskloop add more tools? +
Yes. New tools are added regularly based on what the community finds useful. If you have a suggestion for a tool, send it to hello@deskloop.work. Every new tool follows the same philosophy: one problem, solved cleanly, for free.

Free Productivity Tools No Signup — What Is Deskloop?

Deskloop is a growing collection of free, browser-based productivity tools built for focused, intentional work. Every tool on the platform is completely free to use with no account required, no install, and no data collection. Open any tool directly in your browser and start working in under three seconds.

Who are these tools for?

Deskloop is built for professionals, students, freelancers, and remote teams who need a specific tool right now — without the overhead of signing up for yet another platform. Whether you need a Pomodoro timer for a study session, a meeting cost calculator for a standup, or a time zone overlap finder for a distributed team call, Deskloop has it.

Why free productivity tools with no signup matter

Most productivity software requires an account, charges a subscription, and collects your usage data. Deskloop is built on the opposite philosophy: the best tool is the one you can use immediately, without friction, and trust completely. Every tool stores data locally in your browser and never transmits anything to external servers.

The tools available

Deskloop currently offers seven tools: a Pomodoro-style Focus Timer with ambient sounds, a Habit Tracker with streak protection, a Meeting Cost Calculator with live currency conversion, a Time Zone Overlap Finder with an interactive world map, a Deep Work Tracker with weekly focus scores, a Task Decider spin wheel, and a Work Hours Logger with project breakdowns and CSV export. New tools are added regularly.
