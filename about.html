About

Built for people who just want to get things done.

Deskloop is a free suite of 15 browser-based tools for freelancers, students, and solo workers. Open it, use it, close it. No account. No data leaves your device. No premium tier hidden behind a paywall.

Why Deskloop exists

Most productivity tools are productivity problems. They require an account before you can do anything useful. They sync your data to a cloud you did not ask for. They send notifications, run upsell campaigns, and bury the actual feature you came for under three layers of onboarding.

Deskloop was built as a direct alternative to that pattern. Every tool opens in under three seconds, works without internet after the first load, stores everything locally in your browser, and gets out of your way. No signup wall. No premium tier. No data collection. Focused, functional tools for focused people — and nothing else.

The site started as a single focus timer built for personal use. It grew into 15 tools because the same frustrations kept coming up: productivity software that costs more time than it saves, that asks for your email to access a stopwatch, that breaks when you lose internet. Deskloop fixes all of that by removing the infrastructure entirely. There is no backend. There is no server. There is nothing to break.

Privacy by default

Deskloop has no backend. There is no server receiving your data, no database storing your sessions, and no analytics tracking your behavior. Every piece of data — your habits, your work sessions, your budgets, your streaks — lives exclusively in your browser’s localStorage. Close the tab and it stays on your machine. Clear your browser data and it’s gone permanently. That is exactly how it should work.

The only external requests the site makes are to load fonts from Google Fonts and, if you consent to advertising cookies, to load Google AdSense. Neither of these involves your personal productivity data. AdSense loads only after you explicitly accept cookies on the consent banner — if you decline, no advertising scripts load at all.

There are no user accounts to create, no email addresses to provide, and no passwords to remember. You open a tool and use it. That is the entire relationship.

Who built this

Deskloop is an independent project built and maintained by one person — a BSBA Financial Management student in the Philippines who wanted a set of focused productivity tools without the overhead that comes with every mainstream alternative.

Every tool on the site was designed, coded, and deployed solo. No team, no investors, no office. It is free because it should be free. It collects no data because there is no reason to collect data. The tools exist to be useful, full stop.

If you have feedback, a bug to report, or a tool idea, a real person reads every email sent to this address.
