About

Built for people who just want to get things done.

Deskloop is a free suite of browser-based productivity tools designed around one principle: open it, use it, close it. No account. No loading screen. No data leaving your device. Just the tool, doing its job.

Why Deskloop exists

Most productivity tools are productivity problems. They require an account before you can do anything. They sync your data to a cloud you didn't ask for. They send notifications, run upsell campaigns, and bury the actual feature you came for under three layers of onboarding.

Deskloop was built as an alternative. Every tool opens in under three seconds, works without internet after the first load, stores everything locally in your browser, and gets out of your way. No signup wall. No premium tier. No data collection. Just focused, functional tools for focused people.

The tools

Deskloop currently offers seven tools, each solving one specific problem:

Focus Timer
Pomodoro timer with ambient sounds
Habit Tracker
Daily check-offs and streak tracking
Meeting Cost Calculator
Real-time dollar cost of any meeting
Time Zone Overlap
Best meeting windows across time zones
Deep Work Tracker
Focus sessions with energy ratings
Work Hours Logger
Clock in/out with weekly totals
Task Decider
Spin to pick your next task

More tools are in active development. The goal is a complete productivity toolkit that never costs anything and never asks for anything in return.

Privacy by default

Deskloop has no backend. There is no server receiving your data, no database storing your sessions, and no analytics tracking your behavior. Every piece of data — your habits, your work sessions, your streaks — lives exclusively in your browser's localStorage. Close the tab and it stays on your machine. Clear your browser data and it's gone. That's exactly how it should work.

The only external requests the site makes are to load fonts from Google Fonts and, if you use the Time Zone Overlap tool, to load world map data from a CDN. Neither of these involves your personal productivity data.

Who built this

Deskloop is an independent project built and maintained by one person. No team, no investors, no office. Originally built by a single student who wanted his own focus timer.

Every tool on the site was designed, coded, and deployed solo. It’s free because it should be free. It collects no data because there’s no reason to collect data.
